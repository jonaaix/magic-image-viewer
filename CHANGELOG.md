# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A click on a content image of any web page opens it in a full-screen viewer with zoom at the
  pointer, pan, pinch, keyboard, slideshow, full screen and download; every content image of the
  page forms the gallery.
- Logos, icons, avatars, sprites, tracking pixels and images inside buttons are left alone; the
  largest version a page offers is shown, with fallbacks when a link does not lead to an image.
- A site's own lightbox stays closed; links to other pages keep working and open the viewer with
  Alt-click. Swipeable carousels keep swiping.
- Images opened on their own in a tab are shown in the viewer; "Show in Magic Image Viewer" in the
  context menu opens any image, including images in frames.
- Popup with switches for all sites and for the current site; a grey toolbar icon and badge while
  off.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch and Polish.
